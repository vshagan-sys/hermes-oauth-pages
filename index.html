Hermes — a personal automation tool for one Google account

Private, single-user software. Not offered to the public.

What this is

Hermes is a personal automation tool written and used by one individual, Jay, to work with his own Google account. It is registered in Google Cloud as project hermes-504209. There is exactly one user of this application: its author and operator. There is no sign-up, no account creation for third parties, and no way for anyone else to use it.

What it does

With the account owner's explicit authorisation through Google's standard OAuth consent flow, the tool uses the Google APIs to:

  • list and read files in his own Google Drive, and the Google Docs and Sheets documents among them;
  • read messages in his own Gmail mailbox;
  • read events in his own Google Calendar;
  • build a private, local index of those items so that he can search his own material and keep references to it in one place.

Everything the tool does is for the account owner, on his own machine, on his own data. It does not act on behalf of anyone else, and it does not send, post, share or publish anything.

Availability

This is not a public product or a hosted service. It is not distributed, it is not installed by others, and access to it is limited to the operator's own computer and his own Google account. The pages on this site exist for the two reasons Google requires of an OAuth client: a public home page describing what the application does, and a public privacy policy.
